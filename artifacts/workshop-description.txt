Xenesthesia — Half-Life 2 material effects

DRAFT — NOT UPLOADED. Requires the owner's explicit go before any Workshop item is created or updated.

City 17's surfaces quietly come alive. Brick, concrete, props and supported weapons breathe with restrained, irregular texture movement. Prismatic colours unfold across their surfaces and stay mapped to the textures as you move through the world.

• Gentle surface movement rather than camera blur or screen wobble.
• Material-local colour on compatible walls, props, screens, hands, weapons and NPC materials.
• Keeps the HUD readable and world geometry stable.
• Native game content; no injector or replacement game executable.

For the original Windows Half-Life 2 20th Anniversary edition, using DirectX 9. Other episodes and platforms have not been validated.

Coverage varies by material. Transparent/additive surfaces, special shaders and some animated displays remain unchanged. Objects sharing materials can animate together. Classic zombies and classic headcrabs have an experimental shape pulse, with their original texture colours. Other NPC materials receive colour only; human head deformation is not enabled.

Disable conflicting material addons, including City 17 ASCII, before use. Subscribe and enable through the game's Workshop menu, restart, and start/load from the main menu. These Workshop installation steps still require a clean-install test after publication approval.

To remove all effects, disable the addon and restart. There is currently no instant in-game toggle for native material animation. No keys are bound automatically.
