[h1]City 17 is starting to breathe.[/h1]
Xenesthesia is a psychedelic visual addon for Half-Life 2. Familiar surfaces gently move, prismatic colours unfold across the world, and characters and supplies develop subtle pulses. The effects live on the materials, so they stay with the scenery as you look around.

[h2]What's inside[/h2]
[list]
[*]Restrained, irregular texture movement on compatible walls, floors, stairs, grates and props.
[*]Slowly evolving colour across connected world surfaces, with texture-local colour on supported props, hands and weapons.
[*]Flowing carpets and swirling liquid in Kleiner's lab tanks.
[*]Gentle character and enemy pulses, including a subtle Combine head pulse that follows ragdolls.
[*]Breathing health kits, batteries and wall chargers, plus drifting ink echoes on supported signs.
[*]Native materials and shaders: no injector or replacement game executable. HUD elements stay readable.
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
Subscribe, enable Xenesthesia in Half-Life 2's Workshop menu, then restart the game. Start or load the original Half-Life 2 campaign from the main menu. Disable conflicting material/shader addons, including City 17 ASCII, and remove any older manual Xenesthesia copy first.

Tested on the Windows 20th Anniversary version of the original Half-Life 2 using DirectX 9. Episodes, Linux and Steam Deck are not validated.

[h2]Switching it off[/h2]
Disable the addon in the Workshop menu and restart. No keys are rebound automatically. The developer preview's F5 live toggle uses a separate local helper; it is not included or started by this Workshop addon.

[h2]Coverage and limitations[/h2]
The release covers 2,632 material definitions, with an asset audit across 68 original-campaign maps. This is broad coverage, not a promise that every surface or every scene is affected. Most transparent/additive layers, special shaders and some animated screens stay unchanged. Shared materials can animate together, and some texture/lighting seams remain visible.

Character deformation is experimental. Alyx, Kleiner, selected enemy models and Combine ragdolls received close-up checks; every character, cinematic and detail setting has not been tested. Classic zombies/headcrabs use a different lighting path. First-use shader/asset loading may still cause occasional stutters; performance varies by system and scene.

All listing images are genuine captures of this release build. Still images show the colours and distortion; the texture flow and pulses animate in game.
